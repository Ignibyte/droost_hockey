# R08 — Launch readiness

**Builds:** proof that a staff editor can keep every part of the site current; the XML
sitemap of the eight pages; the staff handover (how to edit each thing, and what is still to
supply); every page held whole to the source at both widths.

**Consumes:** rung 7 (every page and construct the rungs below built).

## Acceptance criteria

- WHEN a user with only the Content editor role signs in, THE SITE SHALL let them add, edit,
  feature and unpublish camps; add, edit and reorder rinks and coaches; edit every Canvas
  page's copy; edit Site details and the main, footer and legal menus; edit and publish the
  legal pages; and read contact-form submissions (Q1); and SHALL NOT let them change the
  theme, modules, roles or site configuration.
- WHEN an editor saves a change, THE change SHALL show to visitors without anyone clearing a
  cache.
- THE XML sitemap SHALL list the eight pages and no camp, rink or coach address (Q9).
- Each of the eight pages SHALL match the source whole at 1280px and 390px wide, except the
  texts set aside by Q3, Q7 and Q8 in droost/parity/decided.json.
- No page SHALL scroll sideways at 390px, and each SHALL have one h1, labelled form fields,
  alt text on its images, and text contrast the source's palette allows.
- THE handover SHALL list what staff still supply before go-live: the phone (Q7), each rink's
  phone and website (Q3), the social addresses and the legal pages (Q8), and the production
  mail settings the contact form's email needs (Q6).
