# R02 — The contact form and /contact

**Builds:** the Contact Webform (adds the Webform module) with Name, Email, Subject and
Message; stored submissions; the email to info@kchockey.com; invisible spam protection; the
subject preset from the address; the /contact page: intro "Hit Us Up", Contact Info (from Site
details), the "Are you a coach?" note, and the "Send a Message" form with its "Message Sent!"
confirmation.

**Consumes:** rung 1 (the frame, Site details, the /contact page shell).

## Acceptance criteria

- THE /contact page SHALL show, as the source does: the "Hit Us Up" intro; "Contact Info" with
  Email info@kchockey.com (a mail link) and Location "Kansas City Metro Area" / "We operate out
  of rinks across KC."; the "Are you a coach?" note; and "Send a Message" with Name, Email,
  Subject and Message and the "Fire Away" button.
- THE Subject select SHALL offer Camp Inquiry, Private Lessons, Rink Information and Other,
  with Camp Inquiry chosen by default.
- WHEN a visitor opens /contact?subject=Private Lessons (or another of the four subjects), THE
  form SHALL open with that subject chosen.
- IF a visitor submits without Name, Email or Message, or with an email address that is not
  one, THEN THE form SHALL refuse it and say which field needs fixing (Q6, Q11).
- WHEN a visitor submits a valid message, THE SITE SHALL store it, email it to
  info@kchockey.com with the visitor's name, email, subject and message, and show the "Message
  Sent!" panel ("Thanks for reaching out. We'll get back to you between periods.") with a
  "Send Another" button that brings the empty form back (Q6).
- WHEN a staff editor opens the form's submissions in the admin, THE SITE SHALL list every
  stored message.
- IF a bot fills the form's hidden spam trap or submits faster than a person could, THEN THE
  SITE SHALL refuse the submission, and no visitor SHALL see a puzzle or a checkbox.
- WHEN staff change the email or location in Site details, THE Contact Info SHALL show the
  change.
