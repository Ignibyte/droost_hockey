# R03 — Rinks: the type, the eight rinks, /rinks and the home page's rinks

**Builds:** Rink (name, city, state, description, amenities, phone, website, sort order); the
eight rinks from the source, with phone and website left empty (Q3); the rink card; the rinks
listing; the map panel; the /rinks page; the home page's "Find Your Ice" section with its three
rinks; record pages forwarding to their listings (rinks here, reused by camps in rung 4 and
coaches in rung 5, Q9).

**Consumes:** rung 2 (the frame, the page shells and the site's patterns as rungs 1 and 2 left
them; the home page shell from rung 1).

## Acceptance criteria

- THE site SHALL hold the eight rinks in the source's order (Pepsi Ice Midwest, Cable Dahmer
  Arena, Central Park Ice Arena, Twin Rinks Ice Pavilion, Olathe Ice Arena, Blade Runners Ice
  Complex, Summit Ice Arena, Swope Memorial Ice Rink), each with its city, state, description
  and amenities, and no phone or website (Q3).
- THE /rinks page SHALL show the "KC Rink Directory" intro, the map panel (the Google Maps
  embed of KC ice rinks, "The KC Ice Map", "Find your sheet.", its text and "Open in Google
  Maps" opening Google Maps in a new tab), then the rink cards two to a row as the source
  shows them: name, "City, ST", description, "Amenities" and each amenity.
- WHILE a rink's phone or website is empty, THE card SHALL show neither; WHEN staff enter
  them, THE card SHALL show the phone as a call link and "Visit Website" opening the site in a
  new tab.
- THE home page SHALL show, after the ticker and the sections above it, the "Find Your Ice"
  section as the source does: heading, text, the first three rinks by sort order with "City,
  ST", their first three amenities and "View Rink" → /rinks, and "Directory of All KC Rinks" →
  /rinks.
- WHEN a staff editor adds a rink, edits one, or changes the sort order, THE /rinks listing and
  the home page SHALL show the change.
- WHEN a visitor opens a rink's own address, THE SITE SHALL forward them to /rinks (Q9).
