- THE /youth-leagues page SHALL show, as the source does: the intro ("Build the next
  generation", "Youth Leagues"); the "Start here" panel ("A team for every stage.", text, three
  checks) beside the "Season snapshot" figures (5 Age pathways, 8+ KC rinks, 1 Great first

  "Adult Leagues"); "A league for every level." with its side note; the three format cards
  (Recreational, Intermediate, Advanced) with level and description; the "Fall season
  spotlight" band (Adult Hockey League Showcase, its text, Sept 14, Twin Rinks Ice Pavilion,
  18+ · All levels, "View event details" → /camps); then "New to the adult game?" with "Start
  with the basics" → /new-to-hockey and "Browse KC rinks" → /rinks beside the four questions.
- WHILE the window is narrow, THE division guide SHALL stack as the source does, dropping the
  detail column.
- WHEN a staff editor edits any figure, division, format, the spotlight or a question in
  Canvas, THE page SHALL show the change.
