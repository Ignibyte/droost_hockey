# R06 — /new-to-hockey: first steps, the FAQ accordion, the checklist

**Builds:** the first-steps cards; the FAQ accordion; the checklist; the question intro with
buttons; the /new-to-hockey page with its six FAQs.

**Consumes:** rung 5 (the page patterns and components the rungs below settled; "Find a
beginner rink" links to rung 4's /rinks).

## Acceptance criteria

- THE /new-to-hockey page SHALL show, as the source does: the intro ("Your first shift starts
  here", "New to Hockey?", the text); the three first-step cards numbered 01–03 (Find the ice,
  Get fitted, Take the first stride); then "The beginner's bench", "Questions? We've got you.",
  the text, the three-line checklist and "Find a beginner rink" → /rinks, beside the six
  questions.
- WHEN a visitor opens a question, THE accordion SHALL show its answer and close any other
  open one; WHEN they open it again, THE answer SHALL close.
- THE accordion SHALL be operable from the keyboard and announce each question's open state
  to assistive technology.
- WHEN a staff editor adds, edits, removes or reorders a question, a step or a checklist line
  in Canvas, THE page SHALL show the change.
