# R01 — The KC Hockey theme, the frame and the page shells

**Builds:** the KC Hockey theme (a sub-theme of droost_theme in web/themes/custom) with the
design's tokens and self-served Oswald and Inter; the header (sticky, the KC Hockey mark, the
main menu, the Contact Us button, the narrow-width menu toggle); the footer (mark, blurb,
social icons, the Explore menu, contact details, copyright, legal links); the main, footer
and legal menus; the Site details block type and its one block; the Terms of Service page
(unpublished) beside the existing Privacy Policy page; eight Canvas pages at the source's
addresses, each with its intro section; on the home page the hero and the ticker; the
not-found page; the site name "KC Hockey" (Q2), metadata and favicon. The Content editor role
gets what this rung builds.

**Consumes:** nothing; the existing Home Canvas page and the existing `main` and `footer`
menus are reused.

## Acceptance criteria

- WHEN a visitor opens any of /, /camps, /rinks, /private-lessons, /new-to-hockey,
  /youth-leagues, /adult-leagues or /contact, THE SITE SHALL answer 200 with the header and
  footer as the source shows them, at 1280px and 390px wide.
- THE header SHALL show the KC Hockey mark linking to /, then Home, Camps & Clinics, Private
  Lessons, KC Rinks, New to Hockey, Youth Leagues, Adult Leagues, then a "Contact Us" button
  to /contact, in that order, and SHALL stay at the top of the window as the page scrolls.
- WHEN a visitor is on a page the main menu links to, THE header SHALL highlight that link.
- WHILE the window is narrower than the source's breakpoint (768px), THE header SHALL show a
  menu toggle that opens and closes the same links and the Contact Us button.
- THE footer SHALL show the blurb, the Explore links (Camps & Clinics, Private Lessons, KC Rink
  Directory, New to Hockey, Youth Leagues, Adult Leagues), "Kansas City, MO / The Hockey
  Capital of the Midwest", info@kchockey.com as a mail link, and "© <current year> KCHockey.
  All rights reserved."
- WHILE the Site details phone is empty, THE footer SHALL show no phone (Q7); WHEN staff
  enter one, THE footer SHALL show it as a call link.
- WHILE a social address is empty, THE footer SHALL hide that icon; WHEN staff set it, THE
  icon SHALL link to it (Q8).
- WHILE the Privacy Policy or Terms of Service page is unpublished, THE footer SHALL not show
  its link to visitors; WHEN staff publish it, THE link SHALL show (Q8).
- WHEN a staff editor changes the blurb, email, phone, location or a social address in Site
  details, THE footer SHALL show the change.
- WHEN a staff editor adds, renames or reorders a main or footer menu link, THE header or
  footer SHALL show the change.
- THE home page SHALL open with the hero as the source shows it: "The Heart of KC Hockey",
  "Kansas City's Ice Headquarters", the paragraph, "Find a Camp" → /camps and "Explore KC
  Rinks" → /rinks, on hero-hockey.jpg; then the gold ticker of the three announcements.
- WHEN a staff editor edits the hero's or the ticker's text in Canvas, THE home page SHALL
  show it.
- WHILE a visitor prefers reduced motion, THE ticker and entrance animations SHALL not move.
- THE headings SHALL draw in Oswald and body text in Inter, served by the site itself.
- WHEN a visitor opens an address the site does not have, THE SITE SHALL answer 404 with a
  not-found page in the site's style, inside the header and footer, linking home.
- Each of the other seven pages SHALL show its intro section as the source shows it (heading,
  eyebrow, text, background), ready for later rungs to fill.
