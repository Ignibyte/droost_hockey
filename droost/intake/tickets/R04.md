# R04 — Camps: the type, the six camps, /camps and the home page's camps

**Builds:** Camp (the existing `event` type, relabelled "Camp or clinic", with start and end
dates, location (a required rink), ages, skill level, price, featured, summary, image and
programs); the Program vocabulary (Youth Hockey, Adult/Coaches, Goalies); the date-range
display; the six camps from the source with their real 2027 dates (Q4) and the featured camp's
photo (Q10); the camps listing with its program filter; the camp card; the /camps page; the
home page's featured camp and upcoming camps; the rink delete guard. Camp pages forward to
/camps by rung 3's mechanism (Q9).

**Consumes:** rung 3 (a camp's location is one of its rinks), and rung 2 (Register opens the
contact form with a subject chosen).

## Acceptance criteria

- THE site SHALL hold the six camps: Summer Power Skating Camp (July 28–Aug 1, Pepsi Ice
  Midwest, 8–14, All Skill Levels, $275, featured), Goalie Clinic (Aug 10, Central Park Ice
  Arena), Back-to-School Skills Camp (Aug 18–22, Cable Dahmer Arena), Fall Puck Development
  Clinic (Sept 6, Pepsi Ice Midwest), Adult Hockey League Showcase (Sept 14, Twin Rinks Ice
  Pavilion, free) and USAH Level 1 Coaching Clinic (Oct 4–5, Olathe Ice Arena), each with the
  source's ages, level and price, dated 2027.
- WHEN a staff editor adds or edits a camp, THE location SHALL be one of the site's rinks,
  chosen from them, and THE camp SHALL not save without one.
- IF a staff editor tries to delete a rink a camp is held at, THEN THE SITE SHALL refuse and
  name the camps held there.
- THE /camps page SHALL show the "Camps & Clinics" intro on camps-drills.jpg, the "Filter By:"
  bar (All Events, Youth Hockey, Adult/Coaches, Goalies) and the camp cards by start date, as
  the source shows them: title, date, the rink's name, "Ages:", "Level:", price, and
  "Register".
- THE date SHALL read as the source writes it: one day as "Aug 10", a range in one month as
  "Aug 18–22", across months as "July 28–Aug 1"; months as "Sept", "Oct", "July" (no year).
- WHEN a camp's price is 0, THE /camps card SHALL show "FREE" and the home page card "Free".
- WHEN a camp is featured, THE /camps card SHALL carry the "Featured Event" band.
- WHEN a visitor chooses a program filter, THE listing SHALL show only camps of that program:
  Youth Hockey shows the first four, Adult/Coaches the last two, Goalies the Goalie Clinic;
  "All Events" shows every camp.
- IF a filter matches no camp, THEN THE listing SHALL show "No camps found" and "Reset Filters".
- WHEN the day after a camp's last day (its end date, or its start date when it has none)
  begins, THE camp SHALL leave the listing and the home page without anyone acting (Q4).
- WHEN a visitor presses "Register" or "Register Now", THE SITE SHALL open /contact with Camp
  Inquiry chosen (Q5).
- THE home page SHALL show, between the ticker and "Find Your Ice", the featured camp as the
  source does ("Featured Camp", title, the summary, Date, Location, Ages, Price, "Register
  Now", the photo with "Level Up."), then "Upcoming Action" with the next three camps that are
  not featured, each linking "Details" to /camps, and "View All Events" to /camps.
- IF no upcoming camp is featured, THEN THE home page SHALL feature the next upcoming camp.
- WHEN a staff editor adds, edits, unpublishes or features a camp, THE /camps listing and the
  home page SHALL show the change.
- WHEN a visitor opens a camp's own address, THE SITE SHALL forward them to /camps (Q9).
