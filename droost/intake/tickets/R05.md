# R05 — Coaches and /private-lessons

**Builds:** Coach (name, specialty, experience, rate, photo, sort order); the four coaches from
the source; the coach card; the coaches listing; the featured instructor spotlight; the
call-to-action band; the /private-lessons page.

**Consumes:** rung 4 (the frame and page patterns the rungs below settled; coach pages forward
to /private-lessons by rung 3's mechanism; Inquire opens rung 2's form).

## Acceptance criteria

- THE site SHALL hold the four coaches in the source's order (Coach Mike Donovan, Coach Sarah
  Tillman, Coach Jake Reardon, Coach Elena Voss) with their specialty, experience and rate.
- THE /private-lessons page SHALL show, as the source does: the "Private Lessons" intro; the
  featured instructor spotlight ("Featured Instructor", "This month at KCHockey", "Level Up.",
  "Power Skating & Edge Work", "Coach Mike Donovan", "Featured rate $75/hr", the bio, the four
  highlights, "Book a session" → /contact); the coach cards four to a row; and the "Need a Coach
  Recommendation?" band with "Get Matched" → /contact.
- Each coach card SHALL show the photo, or WHILE there is none the initials worked out from the
  name ("MD" for Coach Mike Donovan), then name, specialty, experience, "All Ages & Skill
  Levels", "Rate" and "$<rate>/hr", and "Inquire"; WHEN hovered, THE photo area SHALL show
  "View Profile", as designed.
- WHEN a visitor presses "Inquire", THE SITE SHALL open /contact with Private Lessons chosen (Q5).
- WHEN a staff editor adds, edits or reorders a coach, or adds a photo, THE listing SHALL show
  the change.
- WHEN a staff editor edits the spotlight in Canvas, THE page SHALL show the change.
- WHEN a visitor opens a coach's own address, THE SITE SHALL forward them to /private-lessons
  (Q9).
